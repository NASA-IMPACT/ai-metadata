ATLAS/ICESat-2 L3A Land and Vegetation Height V007 (short name ATL08, concept id C3565574177-NSIDC_CPRD) is a NASA Earth-science dataset managed by NASA NSIDC DAAC (archiver, distributor); NASA/GSFC/SED/ESD/I2-SIPS (processor, originator). Its DOI is 10.5067/ATLAS/ATL08.007. Cite it as "ATLAS/ICESat-2 L3A Land and Vegetation Height V007", version 007, published by NASA National Snow and Ice Data Center Distributed Active Archive Center. The data language is eng; usa. It is a NASA standard product. Data were acquired by the ICESat-2 (Ice, Cloud, and land Elevation Satellite-2) platform, an Earth Observation Satellites class platform, carrying ATLAS (Advanced Topographic Laser Altimeter System), 1 instrument(s). Science keywords: EARTH SCIENCE > LAND SURFACE > TOPOGRAPHY > TERRAIN ELEVATION; EARTH SCIENCE > BIOSPHERE > VEGETATION > CANOPY CHARACTERISTICS > VEGETATION HEIGHT. ISO topic categories: CLIMATOLOGY/METEOROLOGY/ATMOSPHERE. Location keywords: GEOGRAPHIC REGION > GLOBAL. Spatial coverage spans -180.0° to 180.0° longitude and -88.0° to 88.0° latitude (horizontal coverage, zone WGS 84 (EPSG:4326), cartesian coordinates, granules represented as geodetic). Horizontal data resolution varies. Temporal coverage runs from 2018-10-14T00:00:00.000Z to present. Temporal resolution is 91 day(s). Quality: processing level 3, version 007, status active. Changes in this version: Removed canopy finding flag in ATL08 regions 7, 8, 9, 10, and 11; Incorporated the CAB delineation of a ground surface to remove noise bands from the ATL03 data product prior to DRAGANN.; Aligned the weak beam to the strong beam from ATL09 based on along-track distance for CAB profile cloud filtering; Updated description for MSW flag; Incorporated a single surface search to improve ground finding; Updated parameters on the quality_ph flag to allow certain photons as input into ATL08; Added a terrain and canopy quality score; Added steps for identifying whether photons are from a single surface or multi-surface; Added ground finding correction based on a priori DEM bias; Added extension of nighttime canopy photons, above FINALGROUND surface; Added steps to reject photons in the presence of fog or low-lying clouds. ATL08 contains along-track estimates of terrain height, canopy height, and canopy cover, as well as beam and reference parameters. Data were acquired by the Advanced Topographic Laser Altimeter System (ATLAS) instrument on board the ICESat-2 observatory. Purpose: Scientific Research.
