ALOS PALSAR Level 1.0 Product (short name ALOS_PSR_L1.0, version 1) is a NASA Earth-science dataset managed by JP/JAXA/EORC Earth Observation Research Center, Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency, Japan (ORIGINATOR); ASF Alaska Satellite Facility (ARCHIVER, DISTRIBUTOR, PROCESSOR). Its DOI is 10.5067/J4JVCFDDPEW1, issued by https://doi.org/. NASA standard product: false. Data were acquired by ALOS Advanced Land Observing Satellite, an Earth Observation Satellites class platform, carrying PALSAR Phased Array type L-band Synthetic Aperture Radar [DSN, FBD, WB2, PLR, WB1, FBS, Arctic, Antarctic].
