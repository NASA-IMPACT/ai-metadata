ATLAS/ICESat-2 L3A Land and Vegetation Height V007 is a NASA Earth-science dataset archived at NASA NSIDC DAAC. Data were acquired by the ATLAS instrument(s) aboard ICESat-2. It measures Terrain Elevation, Vegetation Height. Spatial coverage spans -180.0° to 180.0° longitude and -88.0° to 88.0° latitude. Temporal coverage runs from 2018-10-14T00:00:00.000Z to present. Quality: processing level 3, version 007, status active. ATL08 contains along-track estimates of terrain height, canopy height, and canopy cover, as well as beam and reference parameters. Data were acquired by the Advanced Topographic Laser Altimeter System (ATLAS) instrument on board the ICESat-2 observatory.
